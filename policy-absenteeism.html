Absenteeism & Tardiness

We operate in a team-oriented environment — everyone counts on each other to get the job done. You're expected to report to work on time, observe break limits, and not leave early without prior approval from your supervisor.

Requesting Time Off & Shift Swaps (Sling)

1

Submit time-off requests in Sling as soon as possible — no later than 2 weeks before the date(s) requested.

2

Requests are handled first come, first served. Submitting early gives you the best chance of approval.

3

During busy periods, time off may be denied — be aware of this before booking travel or making plans.

4

Trading shifts with a coworker? The swap must be requested in Sling and approved by the MOD and/or GM before it's official.

5

If no one is found to cover your shift, you are still expected to work it as scheduled. Not showing up will be treated as a no call/no show.

What Counts as an Absence

An absence is any time you're scheduled to work and don't report, or don't complete your scheduled shift. This includes sick days, but not pre-approved paid time off, holidays, or leaves of absence. A partial-day absence is any day you work less than your scheduled shift in a way the company doesn't consider tardy.

Reporting an Absence

  • Notify your supervisor at least 4 hours before your scheduled shift if you'll be absent or more than 30 minutes late.
  • Reporting an absence does not automatically excuse it.
  • Calling out to a coworker instead of your supervisor is not allowed.
  • Failing to notify your supervisor in time is a No Call/No Show and may lead to corrective action, including termination.
Missing two consecutive shifts without notifying your supervisor is considered job abandonment and treated as a voluntary resignation.

Excessive Absenteeism

Even a reported absence can lead to corrective action if it happens too often. Examples of excessive absenteeism:

  • 3 separate absences in any 30-day period
  • 5 separate absences in any 6-month period

A "separate absence" means any full or partial day absent, or one or more consecutive days absent for the same reason. Pre-approved time off for medical/dental appointments, school activities, or personal business (approved before the schedule is posted) won't count as excessive — but it will still be documented as an absence.

The company may request a doctor's note if you're absent more than two consecutive working days within a 7-day period.

Tardiness

You're considered tardy if you arrive more than 7 minutes but less than 30 minutes late, or aren't properly groomed, dressed, and ready to work at the start of your shift. Returning late from a break also counts as tardy. If you're 30 minutes or more late, it's treated as an absence and follows the absence reporting process above.

  • Contact your supervisor personally before your shift starts if you'll be late, unless physically unable — have someone call for you in that case.
  • Reporting your tardiness is required, but doesn't excuse it.
  • Excessive tardiness — even when reported — will result in corrective action up to and including termination.
